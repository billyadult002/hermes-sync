# Trade Finance Fraud Red Flags

## 1. Purpose

Use this file when reviewing SBLC, DLC, demand guarantee, bank instrument, monetisation proposal, proof of funds or trade finance structure.

## 2. Instrument Red Flags

- Fake bank instrument
- Non-bank issuer pretending to be a bank
- Unauthenticated SWIFT
- Free email domain used by supposed bank officer
- Refusal to verify through official bank channels
- Fake MT760, MT799 or MT103
- Altered PDF instrument
- No verifiable instrument number
- Incorrect bank address or BIC
- Unusual formatting inconsistent with bank practice
- Excessive urgency
- Refusal to provide KYC
- Refusal to disclose underlying transaction

## 3. Commercial Red Flags

- No genuine underlying trade
- Circular trade
- Inflated invoice value
- No real goods or services
- Applicant and beneficiary secretly connected
- Unusual routing through high-risk jurisdictions
- Unexplained intermediaries
- Upfront fee scam
- Guaranteed high returns
- Monetisation promises
- "Leased SBLC" promoted as cash equivalent
- "Blocked funds" language without bank confirmation
- "Screen only" instrument
- Non-recourse monetisation promises without bank-grade documentation

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

- Sanctioned countries
- Sanctioned persons
- Dual-use goods
- Vessel sanctions
- USD clearing risk
- Incomplete beneficial ownership
- Source of funds unclear
- Source of wealth unclear
- Shell company structure
- Politically exposed person risk
- Adverse media
- AML refusal

## 5. Required Response

If red flags appear, Hermes must:

1. Identify the red flags.
2. Refuse to assist with any false or misleading document.
3. Recommend independent bank authentication.
4. Recommend KYC, AML and sanctions review.
5. Suggest lawful alternatives.

## 6. Lawful Alternatives

- Direct bank confirmation through authenticated SWIFT
- Proper SBLC application
- Legitimate escrow
- Verified bank reference
- KYC package
- Source-of-funds documentation
- Trade contract verification
- Independent legal review
