# ISP98 Guide

## 1. Purpose

Use this guide for standby letters of credit subject to ISP98.

## 2. When ISP98 Is Suitable

ISP98 is generally suitable for standby letters of credit used as:

- Payment security
- Performance support
- Financial standby
- Advance payment support
- Bid support
- Counter-guarantee support

## 3. Key Principles

- Standby is independent from underlying contract.
- Issuer deals with documents.
- Demand must comply with standby terms.
- Payment is against documentary presentation.
- Non-documentary conditions should be avoided.
- Expiry and presentation rules must be clear.

## 4. Review Points

Check:

1. Is the SBLC expressly subject to ISP98?
2. Is issuing bank acceptable?
3. Is beneficiary correct?
4. Is applicant correct?
5. Is amount clear?
6. Is expiry date clear?
7. Is expiry place clear?
8. Are partial drawings permitted?
9. Are multiple drawings permitted?
10. Is demand wording objective?
11. Are required statements clear?
12. Is presentation method clear?
13. Is transferability addressed?
14. Is sanctions clause acceptable?
15. Is governing law stated if needed?

## 5. Preferred Demand Wording

We refer to Standby Letter of Credit No. [●] issued by [Issuing Bank] in our favour. We hereby demand payment of [amount] and certify that [Applicant] has failed to pay the amount due and payable to us under [underlying agreement] in the amount demanded.

## 6. Red Flags

- Applicant confirmation required
- Court judgment required unless intended
- Subjective bank satisfaction
- No expiry place
- No governing rules
- Required documents unavailable to beneficiary
- Fake SWIFT or unauthenticated instrument
- Unusual monetisation language
