# UCP 600 Guide

## 1. Purpose

Use this guide for documentary letters of credit and commercial letters of credit subject to UCP 600.

## 2. Core Principles

- Credits are independent of underlying contracts.
- Banks deal with documents, not goods or services.
- Presentation must comply with credit terms.
- Documents are examined on their face.
- Issuing bank undertakes to honour complying presentations.
- Confirming bank, if any, adds its own undertaking.
- Applicant disputes generally do not affect bank payment obligation.

## 3. Review Points

Check:

1. Is the credit expressly subject to UCP 600?
2. Is it irrevocable?
3. Are applicant and beneficiary correct?
4. Is amount clear?
5. Is currency clear?
6. Is availability stated?
7. Is nominated bank identified?
8. Is expiry date and place clear?
9. Is latest shipment date clear?
10. Is presentation period clear?
11. Are documents realistic?
12. Are transport documents properly described?
13. Are partial shipments allowed?
14. Is transhipment allowed?
15. Are Incoterms consistent?
16. Is insurance adequate?
17. Are document descriptions consistent with underlying contract?

## 4. Common Discrepancies

- Late shipment
- Late presentation
- Expired credit
- Missing documents
- Inconsistent goods description
- Incorrect bill of lading
- Unsigned invoice
- Wrong beneficiary name
- Wrong applicant name
- Insurance shortfall
- Incorrect endorsement
- Documents inconsistent with each other

## 5. Drafting Preference

Avoid excessive document requirements. Keep conditions documentary, objective and capable of strict compliance.
