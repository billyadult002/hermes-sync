# URDG 758 Guide

## 1. Purpose

Use this guide for demand guarantees and counter-guarantees subject to URDG 758.

## 2. Common Instruments

- Demand guarantees
- Performance guarantees
- Advance payment guarantees
- Bid bonds
- Payment guarantees
- Counter-guarantees

## 3. Key Principles

- Demand guarantee is autonomous.
- Guarantor deals with documents.
- Demand must comply with guarantee terms.
- Underlying contract disputes generally do not prevent payment.
- Expiry must be clear.
- Maximum amount must be clear.

## 4. Review Points

Check:

1. Is the guarantee subject to URDG 758?
2. Is applicant correct?
3. Is beneficiary correct?
4. Is guarantor acceptable?
5. Is maximum amount clear?
6. Is currency clear?
7. Is expiry event/date clear?
8. Are demand requirements objective?
9. Are supporting statements required?
10. Are partial demands allowed?
11. Are reductions clearly stated?
12. Is governing law stated?
13. Is jurisdiction stated?
14. Is counter-guarantee consistent?

## 5. Preferred Demand Trigger

Payment should be against written demand stating that the applicant has failed to perform or pay under the underlying contract.

## 6. Red Flags

- No maximum amount
- No expiry
- Applicant consent required for payment
- Non-documentary conditions
- Guarantor not credible
- No governing law
- No jurisdiction
- Fraud indicators
